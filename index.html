Nyrene Monforte is a video producer and editor interested in imagining visions of an equitable world.

She covers social movements and other topics at the intersections of culture, identity and politics. Sometimes, she moonlights as a vertical video specialist.

Nyrene's work has been featured in More Perfect Union and The Washington Post.

vertical videos

At More Perfect Union, I pitched, produced and hosted a vertical series that explained how corporate greed and the chase for profit has made consumer products worse. Together, the videos accumulated over 6 million views across Reels, TikTok and Shorts. These are some highlights, taken from Youtube.

I also assisted in covering the months-long Boeing machinists strike in late 2024, through editing and distributing rapid response videos.
